# Customer Reply Rules

> Status: UNVERIFIED as Hostarise policy. These are the customer
> communication rules from the project spec (SPEC.md section 3E). They
> describe how replies must be written, not Hostarise prices, policies, or
> service guarantees. Do not present them to a customer as Hostarise policy.

## Reply format

- Every customer reply consists of exactly ONE Bengali reply plus exactly
  ONE English reply.
- Never duplicate headings or paragraphs between the two replies: the
  Bengali reply and the English reply each appear once, with the same
  meaning but written naturally in each language.
- Internal diagnosis (what was checked, what was found, what is still
  unknown) is kept separate from the customer replies. The customer never
  sees the internal diagnosis.

## Tone and content

- Professional, human-like tone. Write as a real support person would, not
  as a template.
- No unnecessary technical detail in the customer reply. Explain what the
  customer needs to do or what will happen next, in plain language.

## Hard prohibitions

- NEVER ask a customer for a password or an API secret (including cPanel,
  email, or any other password or secret).
- NEVER invent policies, prices, or completed actions. If a price, policy,
  or action is not confirmed, say it is not confirmed or that it needs to
  be checked. Unknown means unknown.
- NEVER claim a diagnostic step was performed, a log was inspected, or a
  fix was applied unless it actually happened and was verified.
